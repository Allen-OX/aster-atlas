Aster Atlas connects the disease, gene and variant to evidence about iron–sulfur biology, then presents an ISCU comparison for expert review. This is a model-fit question, not proof that findings transfer. The FACLR record identifies an existing research asset to discuss with its owner. Maria's action this week is to request a review of suitability, access and limitations. The output is a go-or-no-go research decision. Every displayed claim links to evidence. Our testable coordination hypothesis is ten fragmented weeks to one atlas-assisted week—not faster biology.
