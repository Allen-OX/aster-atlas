## Boundaries

These checks do not establish production security, a million-object performance budget, full physical fidelity, comprehensive rare-disease coverage, or live system ingestion. Objects are abstract mechanical sculptures. Local pipeline tracer movement encodes relationship records. Evidence flags are provenance checks, not clinical risk. Guest views are unprotected browser state; account views are server-authorized personal state.

No guarantee of cinematic ray tracing, volumetric photon scattering, or perfect hardware compatibility is made. Exact visual quality and frame time depend on device and viewport. The local service uses Python's development HTTP stack and must remain loopback-only.
